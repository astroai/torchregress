## What I would take seriously for semi-supervised tabular / tabular regression

The most relevant cluster is **not** the segmentation/object-detection line. I would prioritize:

**FreeMatch / SoftMatch / DST / SemiReward** as generic pseudo-label filtering and confirmation-bias baselines; **RankUp** and **Heteroscedastic Pseudo-Labels** for regression; **SimPro / Meta-Expert / adaptive-threshold imbalanced SSL** for label-shift and long-tail effects; and **Complementary Benefits of Contrastive Learning and Self-Training** as a warning that representation learning + self-training only really shines under certain distribution-shift geometries.

The most directly relevant new paper for noisy scientific regression is probably **Semi-Supervised Regression with Heteroscedastic Pseudo-Labels**, because it targets continuous pseudo-label reliability rather than forcing regression into classification. RankUp is also worth testing when target ranking is scientifically meaningful even when absolute calibration is imperfect.
